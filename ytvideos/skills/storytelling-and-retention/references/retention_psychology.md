# Audience Retention Psychology & Algorithmic Hook Engineering

## 1. The 3-Second Cognitive Gate
Neuroimaging and viewer drop-off analytics indicate that the human brain decides whether to commit to a video within **1.8 to 3.2 seconds**. 
The viewer's default state is *threat detection / avoidance of wasted time*. 

### Rules for the First 3 Seconds:
1. **The Paradox Opening:** Present two mutually exclusive truths.
   - *Example:* "This machine can think faster than the entire human race, but it doesn't even know what a number is."
2. **Zero Introductory Latency:** Banned phrases:
   - "Hey guys, welcome back..."
   - "In today's video we will discuss..."
   - "Before we start, make sure to..."
3. **Sonic Punch:** A high-impact sound effect (whoosh, hit, or frequency rise) must trigger within the first 400 milliseconds.

## 2. Pacing Ratios & Pattern Interrupts

```
100% ─────────┐
Retention     │  [Hook]
              │     \    [Pattern Interrupt 1]   [Pattern Interrupt 2]
 50% ─────────┼──────\───────/\──────────────────────/\────────────
              │       \_____/  \____________________/  \___________
  0% ─────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────► Time
              0s     3s       35s                    70s
```

- **Pattern Interrupt Timing:** Every **30–40 seconds**, audience attention exhibits a natural harmonic dip. 
- **Interrupt Mechanisms:**
  - Sudden total audio silence (800ms) right before an impactful claim.
  - Radical color shift (e.g., transitioning from Sumi Light sketch to dark Sumi-e ink brush).
  - Dramatic shift in vocal delivery speed (dropping from 150 WPM to 100 WPM for one sentence).
